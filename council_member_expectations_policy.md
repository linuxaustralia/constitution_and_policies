# Linux Australia Council Member Expectations Policy

## Introduction

Linux Australia is a not-for-profit organisation incorporated in New South
Wales.  The members of the Linux Australia Council are responsible for
managing the organisation, upholding its constitution, and fulfilling
directorship obligations.

## Expectations Of Council Members

Linux Australia Council members should be familiar with the [Linux Australia
constitution](https://github.com/linuxaustralia/constitution_and_policies/blob/master/constitution.md)
and abide by all of [Linux Australia's policies](https://github.com/linuxaustralia/constitution_and_policies)
including the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/linuxaustralia/constitution_and_policies/blob/master/conflict-of-interest-policy.md). 
They are expected to be actively involved in the running of the organisation
and contribute to Council activities.

Members of the Linux Australia Council are expected to carry out the tasks
and activities as per their respective [position description](https://github.com/linuxaustralia/position-descriptions).

Each Council member shall obtain a [Director ID from ASIC](https://www.abrs.gov.au/director-identification-number).  This is a
requirement of ASIC because Linux Australia holds an Australian Registered
Business Number (ARBN).

Linux Australia Council meets online once every two weeks at a time
negotiated by Council members at the start of each term.  Council members
are expected to attend these meetings.  When this is not possible, an
apology shall be sent to the secretary.

Council members should be familiar with Council-related communications
received between meetings.

Linux Australia uses a number of online platforms for documentation and
communication.  These may be self-hosted or hosted by external entities. 
Council members agree to obtain credentials on the platforms as required to
access the resources necessary to carry out their role on Council.

New Council members will be taken through a financial induction by the
Treasurer.

When a person's involvement in the Council concludes, their access to Linux
Australia Council resources may be revoked subject to operational needs.

Linux Australia understands that personal circumstances can change
unexpectedly.  If such changes make it difficult or impossible for a Council
member to carry out the expectations in this policy, the Council member
should discuss the situation with the rest of Council to determine the most
appropriate course of action.

If a Council member fails to comply with these expectations, the situation
will be dealt with in line with governance best practice and the [Linux
Australia
constitution](https://github.com/linuxaustralia/constitution_and_policies/blob/master/constitution.md).
